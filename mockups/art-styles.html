Voxel Sunday

3D pixel art. Everything is built from cubes and rendered at low resolution, so it reads like a pixel sprite you can walk around.

Mood: bright, playful, toy-box.

Suits: arcade play, small teams, easy to add lots of characters and stadiums.

Soft Geometry

Minimalist shapes. Players are smooth pawns, the field is a matte slab, and soft shadows do most of the work.

Mood: calm, sleek, premium.

Suits: tactics and play-calling, where reading the whole field matters more than character detail.
