Prime Time

Stylised realism under stadium lights. Painted turf with yard numbers, a full crowd with camera flashes, four-point floodlighting with crossed shadows, bloom and a TV first-down line.

Style 7

Stop-Motion Clay

Plasticine figures on a tabletop set. Thumb-printed surfaces, rolled-clay yard lines, a macro lens with shallow focus, and animation stepped at 12 frames a second like real stop-motion.

Mood: handmade, charming, funny.

Suits: an arcade game with personality, where big heads and squashy hits are the joke.

Style 8

Pop-Up Paper

A paper-craft diorama. Players are sticker-edged cutouts with split-pin joints that always face you and flip when they change direction. The field, hills, crowd and clouds are layered sheets.

Mood: light, crafty, storybook.

Suits: a casual or family game; characters are 2D art, so new teams and outfits are cheap to make.

Style 9

Watercolour Sketchbook

Full 3D drawn as an ink-and-wash painting. A screen filter traces wobbly ink outlines from scene depth, washes the colour unevenly, pools pigment at edges and leaves a torn white paper border.

Mood: gentle, artistic, memory-like.

Suits: a story-led career game, or anything that wants to stand apart from every other sports title.

Style 10

32-Bit Disc Era

Mid-90s console 3D. Chunky low-polygon players, tiny pixel textures, vertices that snap and wobble, 15-bit colour with ordered dithering, and a curved scanline screen.

Mood: nostalgic, crunchy, arcade.

Suits: a throwback arcade game; very cheap to render and quick to produce art for.
